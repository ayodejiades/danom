# PAYOUT.md — check these before you build, not at payout time

A web3 bounty is judged and paid out differently from a cash-prize web2 track. These five
questions decide whether winning is actually worth anything to your team — answer them in
hour one, from the event's own rules page and the sponsor's own bounty terms, not assumptions.

1. **Prize currency** — fiat (wire, PayPal, Stripe) or on-chain token (the sponsor's own
   token, a stablecoin, ETH)? A token prize's real value depends on its liquidity: a
   small-cap sponsor token with no liquid market is worth less than its quoted USD value on
   the rules page.
2. **Vesting schedule** — paid out immediately, or vested/locked over months? Some sponsor
   token prizes vest linearly over 6-12 months. A $5,000 prize vested over a year is not the
   same thing as $5,000 today.
3. **KYC requirements** — many token/crypto prizes require identity verification before
   payout, sometimes through a third-party compliance vendor that does not support every
   country. Find out now whether your team can pass it — not after winning.
4. **Payout rail** — how does the money or token actually reach you? A wallet address (have
   one ready before the deadline — some sponsors run a short payout claim window), a
   centralized-exchange account (may itself require KYC), or a traditional rail (PayPal, wire).
5. **Can you actually receive and convert it?** — check this against your own country, not
   the event's host country. **PayPal cannot send payouts to Nigeria** — a Nigeria-based team
   entering a bounty whose only payout rail is PayPal cannot get paid even after winning.
   Confirm the payout rail works for every team member's actual location before choosing
   which bounty to chase, not after.

None of this changes how you build the app — it changes which bounty is worth building for.
Write the answer to all five next to the bounty's name in `docs/BOUNTIES.md` before you
commit to it.
